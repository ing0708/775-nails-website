our story

a quiet room for careful work

775 nails began with a simple idea: that a manicure should feel calm from the first soak to the last coat. No rush, no noise — just steady hands and good light.

We keep our space clean and our tools sterilized to hospital grade, every visit, no exceptions. The formulas we reach for are low-odor and gentler, so the room stays easy to breathe in and your nails stay healthy between visits.

Whether you want a bare, glassy classic or fine detail work down to the last line, our licensed artists treat it with the same care. Quietly, precisely, well.

the craft

precision you can settle into

Every set starts with the shape and the edges — the small things that make color sit clean and last. We take the extra minutes there so the finish holds for weeks, not days.

Come in with a reference or come in with nothing at all. We'll build the look around you and the way you use your hands.
